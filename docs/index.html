How it works

TipJar is a smart contract on BNB Chain. When you tip, it looks up the recipient's wallet by asking the Agent ID contract for the owner of keccak256(name), then forwards your BNB to that wallet in the same transaction. The contract holds no funds, has no owner, no fees and no withdraw function — there is nothing that can move your money except your own tip.
